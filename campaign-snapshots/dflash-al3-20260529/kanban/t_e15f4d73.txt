T7 produces a ranked list of optimization candidates. Highest-prior expected wins:

1. **liger_fused_linear_ce = True** — fuses LM head matmul + cross-entropy loss in one kernel, avoids materializing full `[batch, anchors, 200064]` logit tensor. Typical speedup 2-5× on LM-head backward for large-vocab models. Confidence: HIGH on vocab=200064.

3. **liger_rope = True** — fused RoPE apply. Confidence: LOW (RoPE is rarely the bottleneck, but cheap to enable).
4. **AdamW fused/8bit** — `torch.optim.AdamW(fused=True)` or `bitsandbytes.optim.AdamW8bit`. Confidence: LOW for backward-bound regimes, ~5%.

6. **num_workers / persistent_workers tuning** — only if T7's dataloader_next_ms breakdown shows it's a steady-state issue (not just first-batch warmup). Confidence: LOW given GPU is 96% utilized = not data-starved.
