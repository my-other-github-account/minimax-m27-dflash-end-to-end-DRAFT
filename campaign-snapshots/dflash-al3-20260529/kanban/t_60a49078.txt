## WHY THIS MATTERS

The single highest-information experiment on the board. We measure AL=1.363 on our v18 step 2500 drafter. z-lab/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-DFlash publishes AL 3.94–6.34 on standard benchmarks. There are exactly two possibilities:

1. **z-lab's drafter scores its published AL on OUR harness** → our pipeline is fine, OUR DRAFTER is the problem. The path to AL>3 is training (Track A throughput unlocks more training; T11 resume-fix recovers degraded weights; deeper training; possibly architectural changes after that).
2. **z-lab's drafter scores far below 3.94 on OUR harness** → our HARNESS / runtime path is systematically underreporting AL. The path to AL>3 is fixing the measurement instrument first (T12 + further harness audit), not training.

These two paths have NOTHING in common. We need to know which one we're on before committing more compute to either Track A or Track B's downstream fixes.

Training improvements won't show up until we fix measurement. Comment: "harness systematically underreports; AL>3 unreachable until T12 + audit lands".
