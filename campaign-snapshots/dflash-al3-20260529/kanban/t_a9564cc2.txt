# T15: Inference-time AL headroom — what's the absolute ceiling of our v18 step 2500 drafter?

## CONTEXT

T11 tests whether OUR drafter scores its training-side per-pos at runtime. T14 tests whether the HARNESS scores a known-good drafter at its published AL. T15 answers a third independent question: **even assuming perfect measurement and perfect prompts, what's the structural AL ceiling of the v18 step 2500 drafter at inference?**

If the ceiling is well below 3, no amount of harness fixes or distribution matching reaches AL>3 on this checkpoint — we need a better drafter (more training, architectural change). If the ceiling is well above 3, then T11+T12 fixes are sufficient.

## YOUR JOB

### Sweep 1 — draft_max ∈ {3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15}

Run the T16 25-prompt benchmark, varying ONLY draft_max. All other knobs unchanged.

Expected shape per skill `dflash-c15-fp8-hybrid-winning-config-and-health-rules` Rule 6: AL is monotone non-decreasing in draft_max up to a plateau. Plateau height ≈ structural AL ceiling for this drafter on this prompt mix.

Output: `<workspace>/sweep1_draftmax.json` with per-draftmax mean_real_AL + CI.

### Sweep 2 — Greedy AR ceiling for verifier

For each of the 25 prompts, compute:
- Greedy AR token sequence (temperature=0, the verifier-only path).
- "Oracle" speculative AL: at each AR step, simulate offering the AR next token as if the drafter had proposed it; count how many consecutive future tokens the drafter would have to predict-correctly for the verifier to keep accepting under min(1, p_target/p_draft) softmax-ratio acceptance.

This isolates the verifier-acceptance pattern from the drafter prediction quality. The "oracle" speedup is the ceiling IF the drafter were perfect at this prompt.

Output: `<workspace>/sweep2_oracle.json` with per-prompt oracle AL.

### Sweep 3 — Per-position drafter quality vs verifier acceptance

For each prompt, log:
- drafter argmax matches verifier argmax at position k (per-position drafter-quality)
- verifier softmax-ratio acceptance at position k (per-position acceptance even when drafter argmax wrong)
- gap = (1 - drafter argmax) × (verifier softmax-ratio acceptance) — the "drafter is wrong but verifier accepts anyway" path

Theory: at large vocab=200064, softmax-ratio acceptance can save us a lot of positions where argmax doesn't match. Quantify this slack.

Output: `<workspace>/sweep3_perpos_decomposition.json` with per-prompt, per-position triples.

### Synthesis

Block review-required with these specific numbers:
- AL plateau height from sweep 1 (the empirical ceiling at large draft_max)
- Oracle AL from sweep 2 (ceiling assuming perfect drafter quality)
- Per-position softmax-ratio slack from sweep 3 (how much room above argmax-match we have)
